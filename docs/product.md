# Product behavior

Meducation is a local-first study app for quizzes and flashcards. Content comes from PostgreSQL through the API; attempts, scores, checkpoints and progress backups stay in the browser. No account or cloud sync exists for learners.

## Navigation

- A first visit opens a landing page with three sample questions and four sample flashcards, built from the real study components with fixed copies of bank items (`src/app/landing/samples.json`); answers there save nothing. **Start studying** records `meducation.started.v1` and opens the Quizzes dashboard. Later visits, and any browser that already has quiz or flashcard progress, open the dashboard directly. Reset and restore keep the flag; clearing site data shows the landing page again.
- A collapsible left rail has **Quizzes** and **Flashcards**; both open a dashboard built from the shared subject catalog with a Continue Studying carousel. Desktop starts expanded; narrow screens show an icon rail and an overlay drawer. The brand returns to Quizzes.
- Leaving an active quiz or results review asks for confirmation and commits the exit only after the save or clear succeeds. Leaving a flashcard deck saves its checkpoint first.
- Dashboards show subject cards (12 per page, with page controls only when there is more than one page; All or In progress filter) and stat cards. Quiz cards show latest score and trend; flashcard cards show the share of decks completed at least once. Empty catalogs use a short empty state; empty flashcard decks are listed but cannot be launched. Each dashboard keeps its page and filter for the session, and returning from a subject restores its scroll position; other screens open at the top.
- A footer on both dashboards shows the copyright line, **Privacy** and **Terms** (each opens a dialog) and **Contact** (the Telegram support account).
- A subject screen groups its quizzes (or decks) by topic: a header with subject progress (items with at least one completion), a Topics list with per-topic progress, and the selected topic's summary (name, optional description, progress) and quiz or deck list. Only topics with content are listed. Topics and their quizzes (or decks) are ordered by most recent activity (start, resume, answer or finish; Browse excluded), and untouched ones keep catalog order. On wide screens it opens on the first topic; on narrow screens it opens on the Topics list, a topic opens its content, and browser Back returns to the list. Selection is not saved. A search field filters topics and quizzes (or decks) by name, ignoring case and accents: topics whose name or items match stay listed, and a Results view lists the matching items by topic. On wide screens choosing a topic clears the search; on narrow screens it is kept, so Back returns to the filtered list. The query is not saved. Items show their completion count, latest score, relative last activity and, when in progress, the share of questions answered or cards reviewed.
- Continue Studying appears when any subject has an unfinished quiz or deck. It shows one card at a time, ordered by most recent activity, and does not auto-advance.

## Loading and freshness

- The shell and dashboard render before content loads. The subject catalog loads first, a subject's quizzes or decks when selected, and bodies when the learner shows intent (opening quiz setup, pointing at or focusing a deck's Study button) or at launch. Skeletons reserve layout; failures show a recoverable banner or screen-scoped toast with learner-safe copy and never change saved progress. Retries are automatic with a visible countdown and a manual Retry afterward.
- Every explicit launch (start, retake, resume, browse, open or resume a deck, restart confirmation) revalidates the bank's catalog even when bodies are cached, and the check must be under 30 seconds old when study is admitted. Changed revisions or moved membership block the launch with reload guidance; changed saved content requires an explicit restart. Unavailable checks block new launches and preserve saved data. Active study, answers, timers and checkpoints keep their loaded content. There is no polling or idle refresh.

## Quizzes

- One question at a time, with flags, a stopwatch and result summaries. Starting or retaking opens a setup dialog over the subject screen; dismissing starts nothing.
- Each attempt shuffles question order and choice order. The order is kept when you resume, finish and review. Choices are lettered by position. In development builds, a setup switch turns shuffling off.
- **Fast Feedback** locks the selected answer and reveals correctness and the explanation immediately; the stopwatch freezes when every question is locked. **Exam Mode** reveals nothing until submission and never shows live correctness or streaks. Each question has one answer key. Navigator tiles for graded answers (locked Fast Feedback answers, or every answer in Review results) are light green when correct and light red when wrong.
- The quiz and flashcard study headers show the position ("Question 50 of 120"); the progress bar fills by questions answered or cards revealed, with a lighter band marking the position.
- Correct Fast Feedback answers earn a brief burst and an attempt-local streak with celebrations at 3, 5, 10, 25 and 50 that escalate with each milestone; an incorrect answer resets the streak. Scores show 100% only when every answer is correct. A perfect Fast Feedback quiz celebrates once after the last answer, and any perfect score turns the results ring gold. When one answer earns several celebrations, only the highest plays.
- **Browse Answers** is available only on the dev server (`npm run dev`), never in production builds. It is read-only, shows correct answers and explanations, and writes nothing, so it is excluded from activity, scores and trends.
- After a quiz is finished or submitted, **Review results** shows every submitted answer read-only with All, Wrong and Flagged filters and requires confirmation to leave; leaving ends the transient review and keeps the score. Results reveal the score ring first, then the counts, stat cards and actions (see [design system](design-system.md#motion)).
- Left and Right Arrow move through questions in the active quiz, Browse and Review. They never finish or exit, pause while a drawer or dialog is open, and leave answer radios, editable controls and links alone.
- Keys 1-9 or A-I choose the matching answer: Exam Mode selects (changeable) and Fast Feedback submits. They pause like arrows, but a focused answer radio does not block them.
- Saved attempts record a per-quiz content signature. If it is missing or the questions or answers changed, Resume explains and offers an explicit restart; keeping the saved attempt leaves it untouched. Storage failures never advance to a success screen. A stale tab must reload before editing; use one tab per test.

## Flashcards

- The front stays visible above an answer panel. Click, Space or Enter reveals a bold rich answer with its source; Space or Enter on a revealed card advances; clicking a revealed answer hides it. Left and Right navigate from anywhere on the screen (never wrapping), and the last card still requires **Finish**.
- A gold glow builds around the card as you reveal more distinct cards in one sitting, with a faint sizzle past about 20. It resets when you leave the deck and ignores deck length.
- A Cards navigator filters All, Hidden and Flagged in deck order. Opened and flagged cards persist across resume; resume shows the saved card's front. Finish clears the active position and increments the deck's completion count; save-and-exit and restart do not.
- The dashboard shows **Completed**, **Average** and **Highest** (decks finished, decks per local day including idle days, and the best single day). Daily figures begin with the first finish after daily tracking started and keep the local date recorded at finish.

## Progress backup, restore and reset

- **Progress** in the navigation (available when no study session is active) opens one dialog for backup, restore and reset. Backup downloads one gzip-compressed, versioned JSON file (`.json.gz`) with paired quiz and flashcard progress (no running timers; restored timers are paused). It can contain question text, so it is not safe to publish. A download does not prove the file was saved elsewhere.
- Restore accepts only gzip-compressed backups, with a 4 MiB decompressed limit. Choosing a file validates it before anything changes and compares its date, saved quizzes, flashcard decks and total completions with this browser's (omitting that column if current progress cannot be read). Selecting another file clears the previous preview. Closing the dialog stops a pending file read. A warning says restoring cannot be undone. **Restore** replaces both stores after explicit confirmation (never merges), regenerates storage revisions, keeps timers paused, reloads and shows “Progress restored” for five seconds. Empty backups clear progress. Unknown content IDs are kept; resume uses the normal changed-content rules.
- The file is a trust boundary (`progressBackupCodec.ts`): 4 MiB, depth 16 and 250,000 values at most; unknown fields and prototype keys are rejected. Oversized or malformed files are rejected without touching stored progress. Export runs the same validator. Nothing is uploaded.
- Restore writes both stores in turn. If either write fails (for example on quota), the previous values of both are put back and an error says nothing changed. Other open tabs pick up the change through `storage` events and ask to reload when their copy is stale. A crash between the two writes, or a failed rollback, can leave one store replaced; the app cannot detect this, and restoring a known-good backup fixes it.
- **Reset progress** opens a confirmation view that lists the quizzes, flashcard decks and completions to delete, with an optional backup download. Confirming removes both stores (restoring both if either removal fails), reloads and shows “Progress reset” for five seconds. It also works when stored progress cannot be read. With nothing saved, the view only offers Back.
- Clearing site data removes progress and cannot be recovered by the app.
