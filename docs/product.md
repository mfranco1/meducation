# Product behavior

## Learner section navigation

The learner shell has a collapsible left navigation with **Quizzes** and **Flashcards**. Quizzes opens the quiz subject dashboard; Flashcards opens a deck dashboard using the same subject catalog, dashboard grid, and Continue Studying carousel. Flashcards show deck inventory only, with no score or completion analytics. A learner chooses a subject and selects one of its decks directly. Subject screens show the shared concise empty display after their catalog loads successfully with no quizzes or decks; loading and recovery presentations remain visible while data is unavailable. Empty flashcard decks remain visible but cannot be launched. Subject empty displays keep the All subjects navigation available. Subject catalog rows use the shared warm shimmer while loading; selecting Study or Resume shows the orange shell progress bar until the deck opens. A failed deck request leaves its subject list available and shows a persistent, manually closable error toast; selecting Study or Resume retries. Study reveals each card's back on request, saves the current card while navigating, resumes from the saved card with its front visible, and clears the active position only when the learner explicitly finishes. The Meducation brand returns to Quizzes. Desktop navigation starts expanded, while narrow screens keep an icon rail and open an overlay drawer for labels. Navigation from an active quiz or results review retains the existing confirmation and persistence rules; leaving a flashcard deck saves its checkpoint before switching destinations.

## Progress backup and restore

Progress backup is available from the learner navigation when no quiz or flashcard study session is active. It downloads one versioned JSON file containing paired quiz and flashcard progress. Unfinished quiz signatures are preserved and may contain question text and choices; the dialog warns learners to share files only when intended. Downloads are local browser actions and do not prove that a copy was saved off-device.

Choosing a backup reads and validates it before changing storage, then previews its date, saved quiz/deck counts, retained quiz history and durable completion totals. The preview also shows current progress totals and offers a valid current backup before replacement when possible. Restore replaces both progress stores only after explicit confirmation; it does not merge. It creates fresh storage revisions and reloads the app after both stores are verified, announces success and focuses the Quizzes dashboard. A failed or interrupted replacement is gated by a journal and must recover the complete original or imported pair before study resumes. Missing saved keys are valid empty progress; malformed current values are preserved and reported rather than exported as empty. A separate raw recovery copy can preserve the fixed quiz, flashcard and restore-journal values and is never accepted as an ordinary backup. Boot recovery copies are best-effort, may be inconsistent, and are labelled accordingly.

See [backup and recovery](progress-backup.md) for instructions, error handling and diagnosis using copies. Missing file/download APIs disable the affected action with guidance.

Progress remains private to this browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes it. Safe persistence and paired backup require browser Web Locks coordination; browsers without it may browse content read-only when saved progress is readable, while persistent study actions and backup are unavailable. See the [backup acceptance record](work/ongoing/L0-browser-acceptance.md) for the supported-browser and release-check status.

## Flashcard study

Flashcard study keeps the front prompt visible and centered above an orange answer panel. The concealed panel shows a large, centered icon-only Meducation logo; clicking it or pressing Space reveals a centered, bold, rich answer with its source. Space on a revealed card advances to the next card. Left and Right Arrow navigate to the previous and next card from anywhere in the study screen, including when an ordinary button has focus; editable fields, links, arrow-operated widgets, and open dialogs or Cards drawers keep their native keyboard behavior. Navigation stops at the first and last card, and the last card still requires the explicit Finish action. The panel and its answer text share an intrinsic height so the answer fits without clipping through the flip. Clicking the revealed panel hides it; links in the answer remain interactive. Reduced-motion settings make reveal immediate. The top shows a back arrow, card position, and progress without a deck title or timer; Previous/Next sit together at the right. A right-side Cards navigator jumps in canonical deck order and filters All, Hidden, and Flagged. Opening a card means revealing its answer; opening and flagging persist across resume. Navigation conceals the answer on the destination card, and hiding an answer does not clear its opened status. Finish remains explicit on the last card and uses the primary action color; completing a non-empty deck increments its private completion count and clears its active progress together. Leaving or restarting an unfinished deck does not count as a completion. Subject deck cards show `Completed N time(s)` after the first recorded finish, alongside any saved position. Counts begin with finishes recorded after this feature is available; older saves have no completion history.

## Flashcard dashboard statistics

The Flashcards dashboard shows **Completed**, **Average**, and **Highest** above the study carousel and subject grid in the shared dashboard stat-card presentation. Supporting labels identify the units as decks, decks per day, and decks finished in a day. Completed counts every successful deck finish, including repeat finishes, from the durable per-deck completion counts. Average is tracked finishes divided by every local calendar day from the first daily-tracked finish through today, including idle days. Highest is the largest daily deck-finish count recorded since tracking began. Both daily figures start with the next finish after daily tracking is introduced because existing totals have no dates. Average refreshes at local midnight and when the app returns to the foreground. The displayed average uses at most one decimal place and omits a trailing zero. The average and highest use constant-size local progress data; no per-day history is retained. Daily records keep the local date assigned when a finish occurs and are not reassigned after a device timezone change.

## Exam results review

When a selected quiz's questions cannot be loaded, the subject screen does not show an inline failure card. A persistent error toast appears at the bottom right with learner-safe guidance and the quiz name. It stays open through updates on that subject screen until the learner closes it or navigates away; retry by returning to the quiz action. The failure does not alter saved progress. Other notifications can be configured to persist across in-app navigation.

Browse Answers and results review follow the active quiz's question layout and navigation placement while keeping answers read-only. Browse identifies the available correct answer and lets the learner exit directly. Review marks the submitted selection with a checked radio indicator and uses green highlighting for the revealed correct answer (with accessible selection and correctness labels), shows the final elapsed time without running a timer, and displays submitted flags without allowing changes. If an answer or explanation is unavailable, the screen says so explicitly. Review still requires confirmation before exit. In active quizzes and both read-only views, Left and Right Arrow move through questions in canonical order, stop at the first and last question, and never trigger Finish, Submit, Done, or exit. Shortcuts work while ordinary buttons have focus, pause while a question drawer or modal is open, and preserve native behavior in answer radios, editable controls, links, and arrow-operated widgets. Active quiz navigation saves through its normal checkpoint path; Browse and Review remain read-only.

After an Exam Mode submission, the results screen offers **Review results** beside **Back to quizzes**. Review is a read-only view of every submitted answer, its revealed status, and available explanations. The back arrow, Done action, and header logo require confirmation before leaving. Confirmed exit ends that attempt's transient review while retaining its saved score.

The results review question navigator filters items by **All**, **Wrong**, or **Flagged**. Wrong includes answered, incorrect items with answer keys that can be scored; unanswered items and keys under review are excluded. Flagged includes flagged items of every answer status. Filtering affects navigator tiles only and leaves sequential navigation in canonical quiz order. Active quizzes retain **All**, **Open**, and **Flagged**.

Saved tests include a per-quiz content identity. If an unfinished test was saved before identity tracking, or its questions or answers changed, Resume explains that the old question snapshot is unavailable and offers an explicit restart on current content. Keeping the saved test leaves it untouched. Progress writes report browser storage failures without advancing to a success screen; a stale tab asks for a reload before further edits. Learners should use one editing tab per test.

The app shell and dashboard render before content requests finish. The subject catalog fills the dashboard first; each subject's quiz catalog loads when selected, and question details load when the learner starts, resumes, or browses a quiz. Shimmer skeletons reserve the shape of unloaded cards and score values. The completed count remains available from local history immediately; subject-based average and lowest scores resolve automatically once the small subject-to-quiz membership catalog arrives, without opening a subject. A shared catalog failure keeps the dashboard cards and subject-grid footprint visible, with one full-width recovery banner directly beneath the header and the existing shimmering placeholders. A subject quiz-list failure uses the same full-width banner while retaining the subject heading, navigation, and four shimmer placeholders. The banner gives the automatic-retry countdown and can advance its pending retry when the server's Retry-After minimum has elapsed; after retries stop it offers a manual Retry. An independent dashboard region failure stays inside its cards or subject grid. Technical details remain in developer logs. Loading failures do not change saved attempts.

While the learner app or a lazy top-level screen starts, the warm Meducation header stays visible above the Meducation icon, a short loading bar, and one concise status line. Ready content replaces the status immediately; reduced-motion settings disable its short fade and keep the bar static. The local content admin uses the same presentation while its question-bank snapshot loads.

Dashboard active-study behavior: when at least one subject has an unfinished quiz or flashcard deck, show a user-controlled, wraparound Continue Studying carousel below the dashboard statistics. Quiz subjects are ordered by most recently saved active-test activity (canonical subject order breaks ties); flashcard subjects retain their provided active-activity order. Cards show the subject, the number of quizzes or decks in progress, and Continue. Selecting any part of a card opens that subject, where the learner can resume an existing session or choose another activity. The carousel shows exactly one full card at a time at every available width, including on wide screens; subsequent cards are reached through carousel navigation. Keep previous/next controls only when the carousel has multiple reachable positions. Keep the complete subject catalog below the carousel, and do not auto-advance it. Empty catalogs and empty In progress filters use the shared concise empty-state pattern; an empty active filter offers Show all subjects. Previous/Next catalog pagination slides horizontally in the direction of travel. Filter or catalog updates replace pages immediately, and reduced-motion preference disables the slide.

The app supports one-question-at-a-time quizzes, flags, a stopwatch, immediate feedback and deferred exam feedback, result summaries, and metadata analytics. Starting or retaking a quiz opens its Learning Mode setup in a modal over the subject screen; dismissing it starts nothing, while an active attempt resumes directly. Learning Mode offers Fast Feedback: selecting an answer locks it and immediately reveals correctness and feedback. Once every question has a locked answer, the stopwatch freezes; learners can review questions and feedback without adding to their completion time. Leaving and resuming a fully answered Fast Feedback attempt keeps the clock frozen. **Browse Answers** opens a read-only view with the correct answer and explanation shown for each question; learners can move through questions and leave directly. Browsing does not create or update an attempt and is excluded from quiz activity ordering, completions, scores, trends, and dashboard statistics. Every newly committed correct answer receives a brief, local green-circle burst; incorrect answers and answer keys under review do not. Correct Fast Feedback answers build an attempt-local streak in answer-commit order; an incorrect committed answer resets it and lets the learner earn streak celebrations again. A small non-blocking celebration appears at 3, 5, 10, 25, and 50 correct answers in a row. Streak progress survives leaving and resuming, but Exam Mode never exposes a live streak, correctness, or success animation before submission. The final **Finish** or **Submit** action opens a confirmation dialog; only an explicit **Submit** completes the test, while cancelling returns the learner to their unchanged quiz. Completing a non-empty test with every answer correct shows a distinct perfect-test celebration on the results screen in either mode; this uses exact correct and total counts, not the rounded percentage. Attempts are private to the browser via localStorage. Within a subject, quizzes appear by most recent use; never-used quizzes follow in their canonical order. Dashboard progress focuses on each subject's most recent completed score: it shows the equal-weighted average of those scores, their lowest value, and each subject's latest score. The lowest-score card names the subject with that score; ties use the subject whose latest score was completed most recently. When the latest and immediately previous completed scores are available, a subject card also shows a green upward triangle for an increase, a red downward triangle for a decrease, or a horizontal line in the latest-score text color when they are equal. A subject card also displays a compact, text-labelled count of unfinished tests when the subject has any saved active attempts. A subject's test cards show completion count and latest score with an optional comparison to its immediately previous completed score. The stopwatch counts only while the learner is on an active quiz screen: leaving, reloading, or closing an incomplete test pauses it, and resuming continues from the saved elapsed time. A fully answered Fast Feedback attempt stays paused during review, leave, and resume. A back arrow above the question opens one exit dialog where leaving saves progress, aborting discards it, and dismissing the dialog continues the test. Learners can leave an in-progress test without discarding it and later resume at the last viewed question; aborting a test discards that saved progress. The header logo opens Dashboard directly from Browse Answers. During an active quiz it opens the exit dialog first; a confirmed leave or abort then opens Dashboard, while the quiz back arrow continues to return to the subject screen.
