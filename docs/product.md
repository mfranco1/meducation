# Product behavior

Dashboard active-study behavior: when at least one subject has an unfinished test, show a user-controlled, wraparound “Continue studying” carousel directly below the Subjects heading. It contains only those subjects, ordered by their most recently saved active-test activity (canonical subject order breaks ties); selecting a card opens that subject. Keep the complete subject catalog below the carousel, and do not auto-advance it.

The app supports one-question-at-a-time quizzes, flags, a stopwatch, immediate feedback and deferred exam feedback, result summaries, and metadata analytics. Starting or retaking a quiz opens its Learning Mode setup in a modal over the subject screen; dismissing it starts nothing, while an active attempt resumes directly. Learning Mode offers Fast Feedback: selecting an answer locks it and immediately reveals correctness and feedback. Every newly committed correct answer receives a brief, local green-circle burst; incorrect answers and answer keys under review do not. Correct Fast Feedback answers build an attempt-local streak in answer-commit order; an incorrect committed answer resets it and lets the learner earn streak celebrations again. A small non-blocking celebration appears at 3, 5, 10, 25, and 50 correct answers in a row. Streak progress survives leaving and resuming, but Exam Mode never exposes a live streak, correctness, or success animation before submission. The final **Finish** or **Submit** action opens a confirmation dialog; only an explicit **Submit** completes the test, while cancelling returns the learner to their unchanged quiz. Completing a non-empty test with every answer correct shows a distinct perfect-test celebration on the results screen in either mode; this uses exact correct and total counts, not the rounded percentage. Attempts are private to the browser via localStorage. Within a subject, quizzes appear by most recent use; never-used quizzes follow in their canonical order. Dashboard progress focuses on each subject's most recent completed score: it shows the equal-weighted average of those scores, their lowest value, and each subject's latest score. The lowest-score card names the subject with that score; ties use the subject whose latest score was completed most recently. When the latest and immediately previous completed scores are available, a subject card also shows a green upward triangle for an increase, a red downward triangle for a decrease, or a horizontal line in the latest-score text color when they are equal. A subject card also displays a compact, text-labelled count of unfinished tests when the subject has any saved active attempts. A subject's test cards show completion count and latest score with an optional comparison to its immediately previous completed score. The stopwatch counts only while the learner is on an active quiz screen: leaving, reloading, or closing a test pauses it, and resuming continues from the saved elapsed time. A back arrow above the question opens one exit dialog where leaving saves progress, aborting discards it, and dismissing the dialog continues the test. Learners can leave an in-progress test without discarding it and later resume at the last viewed question; aborting a test discards that saved progress.
