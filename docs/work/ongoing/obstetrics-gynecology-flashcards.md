- Medical Disorders in Pregnancy: free T4 target on antithyroid drugs (ATA 2026 full text read has no numeric target); PE imaging (ESC 2025 text truncated, ACOG PB 196 paywalled); methadone or buprenorphine and avoiding withdrawal (ACOG CO 711 summary only); pyelonephritis admission with IV antibiotics and nitrofurantoin or fosfomycin exclusion (ACOG 2023 blocked, NICE NG111 partial); toxoplasma IgM alone; MCA Doppler for fetal anemia; "major aortopathy" in mWHO IV.
- Reproductive Endocrinology: SSRIs first-line for PMDD (ACOG CPG 7 summary only, RCOG GTG 48 on Wiley); NSAIDs first-line for primary dysmenorrhea (SOGC 345 challenge pages, ACOG CO 760 withdrawn); LNG-IUD for endometrial protection in anovulatory PMOS (kept; 2023 guideline names only progestogens, COCs and POPs; ACOG PB 194 unread).
- Gynecology: red degeneration and its conservative management in pregnancy, rapid growth not diagnosing leiomyosarcoma, GnRH analogues and add-back (ACOG PB 228 members-only, SOGC 461 abstract only); Bartholin biopsy at ≥40, Word catheter or marsupialization, no routine antibiotics (narrative reviews only); staphylococcal TSS management (CDC clinical pages 404).
- Gynecologic Oncology: WHO 2021 screening from 30 every 5 to 10 years (full text unreachable); ACOG cytology every 3 years at 21 to 29 (Committee Statement 28, paywalled); no routine endometrial biopsy in asymptomatic tamoxifen users (ACOG CO 601 unreadable, reaffirmation unconfirmed); dVIN as HPV-independent (ISSVD summary only, WHO 2020 unreachable).
