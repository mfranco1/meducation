## Scope

## Decisions

- Test current Philippine law only. Don't test or mention superseded rules (the older textbook-era versions the handout lists beside current law).
- Deconfliction follows the existing topic descriptions: hospital deposit/detention, data privacy and records go to Liability and Patient Rights; child abuse, VAWC and rape law to Sexual, Reproductive and Forensic Psychiatry; evidence types to Legal Medicine; RA 9165 to Toxicology; PMA code to Medical Ethics.

## Subject notes (for `SUBJECT_NOTES`)

- Test current Philippine law only. Never write superseded rules into stems, choices, rationales or pearls, even as "formerly". The handout often lists an old rule beside the current one; use the current one.
- The handout has some wrong answer keys and mistakes in its own practice questions. Verify legal facts (penalties, distances, terms, deadlines) in the primary text: the Official Gazette, LawPhil, Chan Robles, PRC or DOH sites, and the 2019 Revised Rules on Evidence.
- `src/addendum.txt`, `src/buzz.txt` and `src/cards.txt` are supplementary; grep them for your subtopics.
- Laws are a recall-heavy area: keep Recall at or under 30% by writing short scenarios (a physician, a hospital, a court situation) that ask what the law requires or allows.
